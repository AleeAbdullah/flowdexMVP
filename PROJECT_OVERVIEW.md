# FlowDex Project Overview

## Product Summary

FlowDex is a wallet-first digital asset platform currently focused on the public launch and purchase of the FlowDex token. The delivered application combines an informative public website, a non-custodial multi-chain checkout, wallet-based purchase records, and an administrative payment-monitoring area.

The experience is designed to let participants research the project and complete a purchase without transferring control of their wallet to FlowDex.

## Public Website

The public website introduces the FlowDex vision and guides visitors through the project before they participate. It includes:

- A responsive landing experience with launch information and key project highlights.
- Dedicated About, Tokenomics, Roadmap, FAQ, Updates, Blogs, and Whitepaper pages.
- Terms, Privacy, and Legal pages that keep important notices easy to access.
- Light and dark display themes, mobile-friendly navigation, and clear calls to action.

## Token Purchase Experience

The buy page provides live presale information, including the current tier, token price, next-tier price, funds raised, tokens sold, and sale progress. Buyers can enter a desired token amount, review the calculated payment, connect a compatible wallet, and approve the transaction directly from that wallet.

The current checkout supports:

- Ethereum payments using ETH.
- Solana payments using SOL.
- Bitcoin payments using BTC.
- TRON payments using USDT TRC20.

Wallet options vary by network and include supported MetaMask experiences, Xverse for Bitcoin, and TronLink as a TRON option. FlowDex does not take custody of the buyer’s wallet or private keys.

During checkout, the application checks the selected network and wallet address, prepares the payment request, records the submitted transaction, and follows its confirmation status. The interface communicates pending, detected, confirming, confirmed, expired, failed, underpaid, overpaid, and late-payment outcomes.

## Portfolio and Purchase Records

Participants can connect a wallet or enter a wallet address to view its presale activity. The portfolio presents invested value, confirmed token allocation, estimated values, average entry price, transaction totals, payment status, and confirmation information.

A separate transaction history and receipt experience provides wallet-specific records such as network, asset, amount, recipient, transaction reference, block number, and relevant dates. Receipt access is protected through wallet verification.

The buy experience also includes a leaderboard based on confirmed purchases.

## Administration and Operations

The protected admin area gives the FlowDex team an operational view of the sale. It provides total, pending, confirmed, and failed payment counts; confirmed volume; volume by chain; recent activity; and a lifecycle-status breakdown.

Administrators can filter payment records by status, chain, asset, sender, receiver, or date range and inspect individual transaction details, including amounts, addresses, confirmations, timestamps, and available payment data.

## Current Scope and Direction

The delivered product centers on project presentation, token purchases, payment tracking, portfolio visibility, and administrative oversight. Trading, staking, referrals, governance, broader global-market access, and FlowChain are presented as future product directions and are not included as completed functionality in the current release.
